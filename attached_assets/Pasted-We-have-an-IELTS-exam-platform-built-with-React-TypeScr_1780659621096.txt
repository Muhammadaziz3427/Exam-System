We have an IELTS exam platform built with React (TypeScript, Tailwind), Express backend, Supabase database. The student exam page (StudentExam.tsx) currently supports dynamic JSON-based tests with listening, reading, writing sections. We need to modify the exam flow to match the following exact behavior:

## Required Exam Flow

### Listening Section
- The listening section contains an audio player that:
  - Has ONLY a "Play" button (no pause, no seek, no volume control).
  - When clicked, the audio starts playing and cannot be stopped.
  - No progress bar or time display (or very simple display without seeking).
- When the audio ends, automatically start a 2-minute "transfer time" (review time).
- During these 2 minutes, the student can review and edit their listening answers.
- A visible countdown timer (2:00 to 0:00) is shown.
- After the 2 minutes end, automatically move to the Reading section.
- The student cannot manually go to Reading before the transfer time ends.

### Reading Section
- Timer starts at 60:00 (60 minutes).
- The student can navigate between reading passages and questions (already implemented).
- When timer reaches 0:00, automatically move to Writing section.
- Student cannot manually go to Writing before timer ends (or can, but with a warning? Better to enforce auto transition).

### Writing Section
- Timer starts at 60:00 (60 minutes) covering both Task 1 and Task 2.
- When timer reaches 0:00, automatically submit the entire exam.
- Before auto-submit, show a warning modal: "Time is up! Your exam will be submitted now."
- After submission, redirect to a "Thank you" page or show results.

### Submission & Scoring
- Listening and Reading answers are auto-graded on the backend (already implemented in routes.ts).
- Writing answers are saved as text (not auto-graded). Teacher will grade later.
- Speaking is offline – teacher will enter score later via admin panel.
- Final exam submission stores all answers (listening, reading, writing) and sets status = 'completed'.

## Admin & Teacher Grading Flow

1. **Teacher Panel** (`/teacher` route, accessible only to users with role='teacher'):
   - Show list of completed exam sessions with student name, email, exam title.
   - Click on a session to view:
     - Writing Task 1 and Task 2 answers (read-only text).
     - Input fields to enter Writing score (0-9 band, e.g., 6.5) and Speaking score (0-9).
   - Save scores to `submissions` table (or `exam_sessions` table add columns: `writing_score`, `speaking_score`).

2. **Admin Panel** (existing admin-exams.tsx simplified):
   - Show all completed sessions with student details, auto-graded listening/reading scores, and teacher-graded writing/speaking scores.
   - Calculate overall band (average of 4 skills).
   - Button "Send Results to Students" – sends an email to each student with a breakdown of their scores (listening, reading, writing, speaking, overall band).
   - Use the existing email sending function (nodemailer).
